Mood Cookies

By: Trushti S.
Servings

8-10

Prep Time

10 min

Cook Time

12-15min

Ingredients

  • ½ cup soft butter (room temp)
  • ½ cup brown sugar
  • ¼ cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 ¼ cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup chocolate chips (be generous—the mood demands it)
  • Pinch of sea salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350ºF (175ºC).
  2. Mix the softened butter & sugar until creamy.
  3. Beat in the egg and vanilla and mix again.
  4. Add the flour, baking soda, and salt. Mix gently until combined.
  5. Throw in the chocolate chips (be generous—the mood demands it).
  6. Scoop messy little balls onto a baking tray.
  7. Bake for 9-12 minutes until the edges are golden.
  8. Eat one warm. (Let the rest fit your mood ideally)

These cookies were born out of a messy mood.
No measuring cups. No plan.
Just vibes—and way too many chocolate chips.
They came out uneven but soft inside, exactly like I felt.
And honestly? They made the day taste a little sweeter.
