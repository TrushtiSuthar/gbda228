Lonely Noodles

By: Trushti S.
Servings

1 Bowl

Prep Time

5 Min

Cook Time

10 Min

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of instant ramen/noodles
  • 1 egg, fried or soft-boiled
  • A handful of cherry tomatoes, halved
  • A few slices of cucumber
  • Soy sauce and chili oil to taste
  • 1 spring onion, chopped

Instructions

  1. Boil water and cook the instant noodles. Drain, reserving about 1 cup of cooking water.
  2. Add the spice/flavor packet to the reserved water in the pot. Stir to dissolve.
  3. Add the cooked noodles back to the pot with the broth. Stir in soy sauce and chili oil.
  4. Transfer the noodles and broth to a serving bowl.
  5. Top with the fried egg, cherry tomatoes, cucumber slices, and spring onion. Serve immediately.

I made these noodles on a night when the apartment felt too quiet. Even the microwave felt louder than me. I wasn't really hungry—just needing for something warm. The steam from the pot made the room feel less empty. Not perfect, not fancy... but enough.
