Quich Chai

By: Trushti S.
Servings

1-2 cups

Prep Time

2 min

Cook Time

5-7min

Ingredients

  • 1 cup milk (or water)
  • ½ cup water
  • 1-2 tsp tea leaves (or 1 tea bag)
  • ½ tsp sweetener (or sugar)
  • ½ tsp spices (cinnamon stick or pinch of ground cardamom)
  • Round slice of ginger, crushed (optional, but needed)

Instructions

  1. In a small pot, pour water and milk together.
  2. Add the tea leaves, sweetener, and ginger.
  3. Let it simmer until the color deepens—at least 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add sugar and stir until it dissolves.
  5. Strain into your favorite mug.
  6. Drink while standing in the kitchen, thinking today, I've got this."

This chai wasn't planned.
Just a rushed morning rescue.
But the moment the cinnamon cracked in the pan, everything slowed down.
One sip, and suddenly the day felt like it wasn't going to win after all.
